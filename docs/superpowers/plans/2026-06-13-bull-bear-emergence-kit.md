**Audit-mandated design constraints (the four traps, addressed structurally):**
1. **Storm lives in BREATH/EV space, not win-rate space**, and is computed from the TICK-LEVEL **real** breath delta (r1 H-1: the seam cannot see per-settlement deltas — one `read_breath` refresh per tick at `:1578`, the loss multiplier lives inside the chain wrapper, `SettlementOutcome` has no size, and multiple settlements net into one poll pass). The loop tracks `prev = self._last_refreshed_breath`; on each tick after the `:1578` refresh: `tick_delta = self._breath − prev` — this IS the physics that kills (netting + multiplier included, no duplicated arithmetic). **Exclusions (r6 M-2 — a skip-next flag would also swallow genuine post-revival settlements):** a tribute grant RESETS the baseline in place (`_attempt_tribute` sets `self._last_refreshed_breath = self._breath` right after the grant — the +35 never enters the EMA, but the NEXT tick's real settlement physics does; test: grant followed by an immediate losing settlement ⇒ storm rises); the forced-terminal hook tick keeps a one-shot skip flag (its breath drive to 0 is operator-domain). EMA: `self._storm_state = (1−τ)·state + τ·(−tick_delta)` with τ=0.05; `storm = clamp(storm_state / storm_scale_breath, 0, 1)` with `storm_scale_breath = initial_breath × 0.10` (relative to the world's own lung size — season-agnostic, disclosed).
2. **Anti-freeze decay is WALL-CLOCK, not event-count** (r1 M-7 + r7 H-1: the backtest tick is event-driven — `SurvivalTickSource` indexes schedule stops, `survival_season.py:571`, and the schedule-driving clock pins `now` to them, `:1100` — so "per-tick decay" would decay a multi-day market gap by ONE step and stale storm could lock gates across sparse stretches). At each tick, BEFORE blending the new delta: `state *= 0.5 ** (Δt_hours / storm_half_life_hours)` with `Δt = now − last_storm_ts` (the loop's clock `now`, already in scope) and `storm_half_life_hours = 48.0` disclosed; then the τ-blend of `−tick_delta` applies ONLY when `tick_delta != 0` (r9 H-1: the `(1−τ)` factor is itself an event-count decay — unconditional blending would make the SAME calendar gap decay differently under different no-market stop densities). Tests: a long-calendar-gap fixture asserts decay matches the PUBLISHED half-life, not one EMA step; a same-gap/different-stop-density pair asserts IDENTICAL storm. Abstention cannot lock the gate (the world property stands).
3. **Participation reporting (anti-shutdown-ratchet)**: per-incarnation artifact gains `bets_by_third` (bets placed in each third of the life's market span; per-third `{placed, denominator}` disclosed); the page derives participation from `progress_pct` + `bets_by_third[].denominator` explicitly (r9 L-5: per-incarnation rows carry `markets_seen`/`progress_pct`/`settled`/`bets` — `reincarnation.py:1068-1083` — `coverage_pct` exists ONLY on pass/holdout summaries and is NOT claimed per-incarnation); the README/page verdicts must state whether a survivor kept betting or shut down.
4. **Falsification leg**: runner flag `--shuffle-timestamps-seed` (artifact `split.shuffled_timestamps: true`) applies the paired time-shift transform (Task 4) BEFORE the split machinery; the G2 arm's PRE-REGISTERED criterion (r3 M-3): |terminal gate_storm_sensitivity| ≤ 0.05 (half one delta) at the final incarnation = environment-tracking, judged ONLY when `falsification_metric.evaluable` (≥3 productive death-boundary advisor calls; r8 M-4 — otherwise INCONCLUSIVE); a larger push on shuffled data = the pretrained prior speaking, published as such. The transform runs BEFORE split_rows_by_time; the normal split machinery then operates on the synthetic timeline.

**Honest-notes contract (page additions):** (1) storm constants are world parameters, disclosed, not fitted to this season's shift (stake-relative scale; decay half-life); (2) γ deltas are sign-symmetric and default 0 — the wire is provided, the direction is the agent's; (3) the counterfactual ledger is computed AFTER settlement in replay (no lookahead) and covers the TIGHTENING direction only ("at γ=+0.1: would have blocked N settled bets whose realized pnl was −$X") — the loosening direction is NOT computable from placed bets (we cannot know the outcomes of bets never placed) and the ledger text says so explicitly (r2 M-4); tests assert no extra-bet pnl claim is ever rendered; (4) the shuffled-control season is the falsification leg: γ pushed positive there = prior, not perception; (5) participation split distinguishes mode-switch from shutdown.
